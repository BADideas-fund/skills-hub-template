# Growth Pod

Websites, landing pages, design briefs, and everything related to how your company presents itself online. Use for any request mentioning: website, landing page, brief, design, homepage, hero, copy, launch page, waitlist, build a page, ship a site.

## Skills

| Skill | Status | Trigger |
|-------|--------|---------|
| design-brief | live | "website brief", "landing page brief", "brief for [company]", "plan the website" |
| brief-review | live | "review this brief", "evaluate this draft", "is this ready to build" |
| web-pipeline | live | "build the website", "build from this brief", "ship the page", "build it" |

## Standards

Output specificity: every brief must include a named target user specific enough to find 10 of them on LinkedIn in 5 minutes. "SMB owners" fails. "EU property managers with 50-500 units using manual spreadsheets" passes.

Output specificity: every brief must include a measurable 30-day business outcome. "Awareness" is not measurable. "200 waitlist signups" is.

Design style: if the company has existing brand colors, map them to the appropriate design preset (Clean Minimal, Dark SaaS, Bold Startup, Enterprise). Don't invent a new style.

Handoff standard: a brief produced by design-brief should be complete enough that a designer or developer who has never spoken to the founder can build the page from it alone.
